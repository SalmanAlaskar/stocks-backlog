## Notes

- SABIC reports both a "reported" (IFRS) net income figure and a company-defined "adjusted" net income figure that strips out non-operational items and one-offs. For FY2025, these diverge dramatically: a reported net **loss** of SAR 25.78 billion vs. an adjusted net **income** of SAR 2.07 billion. Per the company, the large reported loss stems from portfolio-optimization actions — specifically the reclassification of the European Petrochemicals business and the Americas/Europe Engineering Thermoplastics business as "held for sale," along with related fair-value adjustments and impairments (approximately SAR 21.33 billion), as disclosed on the Financial Review page of SABIC's 2025 Integrated Annual Report.
- All SAR/USD conversions in the source documents use a rate of 3.75.
- Balance-sheet figures (total assets/liabilities/equity) were only found for FY2025 (annual); the corresponding quarterly (Q2 2026) balance-sheet detail beyond net debt was not found in the source reviewed — reported here as "Not found in available sources" for that line item.
- Q1 2026 adjusted net income (SAR 816 million, adjusted EPS SAR 0.27) was reviewed as a cross-check but is not the "most recent" quarter since Q2 2026 results are now available; it is not included above as a primary figure.
- Given the scale of one-off items (divestitures, impairments) affecting FY2025 and the swing from profit in Q1 2026 to loss in Q2 2026, figures should be treated as time-sensitive; consult SABIC's official filings (Tadawul/Saudi Exchange disclosures and SABIC Investor Relations) for the latest updates and full audited statements.
