## Notes

- **Ticker mismatch (important):** The task specified "TASI ticker 3030" for Saudi Ceramic Company. Direct verification against Argaam, Mubasher, StockAnalysis, TradingView, and Google Finance shows ticker **3030 is Saudi Cement Company**, a different building-materials issuer. Saudi Ceramic Company's real Tadawul code is **2040**. All data in this file is for Saudi Ceramic Company under ticker 2040. Anyone wiring this into the demo app under symbol "3030" should either relabel it to 2040 or clearly flag the mismatch, since real trading platforms would reject/mismap a "3030" lookup expecting Saudi Ceramics.
- **FY2024 was a loss year:** Saudi Ceramic Company recorded a net loss in FY2024 (~SAR 71.9 million attributable to shareholders) due to a fire incident at one factory (SAR ~165 million provision impact cited in company disclosures), an impairment of ~SAR 78 million at subsidiary Ceramic Pipes Company, and elevated raw-material/financing costs. FY2025 shows a strong recovery, partly aided by one-off items (e.g., an SAR 120 million insurance compensation recognized in Q2 2025, and a partial reversal of the prior PP&E impairment).
- **One-off items affect YoY comparability:** Q2 2025 included non-recurring items (~SAR 131.2 million combined: SAR 120 million insurance claim settlement, SAR 3.5 million PP&E impairment reversal, SAR 7.7 million zakat adjustment). This makes the H1 2026 vs. H1 2025 net-income decline (down ~68%) look worse than the underlying operating trend — Argaam's Q2 2026 commentary notes that excluding non-recurring items, net profit actually improved by ~SAR 38.9 million YoY.
- **Quarterly figures for 2026 are less rigorously sourced than the FY2025 annual figures.** The FY2025 figures above come directly from the company's own audited Annual Report 2025 PDF (statement of financial position and statement of profit or loss, in SAR thousands). The Q1/Q2 2026 figures come from secondary aggregators (Sahm Capital, StockAnalysis.com) and an Argaam news article, because the primary interim financial-statements PDF for the six-month period ended 30 June 2026 could not be located/fetched directly during this research session (search engines confirmed the Saudi Exchange announcement exists, but the linked PDF document itself was not retrievable). Treat the Q2 2026 revenue/net income/EPS figures as approximate pending verification against the primary filing.
- **Smaller-cap, less-covered stock:** Saudi Ceramic Company is a mid/small-cap Tadawul industrial name (~100 million shares outstanding, market cap roughly in the low single-digit billions of SAR). Coverage from English-language financial media and data aggregators is sparser and sometimes less precise than for large-cap TASI names (e.g., Aramco, SABIC), so cross-checking multiple sources was necessary and some figures (particularly Q2 2026 standalone numbers) could not be independently confirmed against a primary-source PDF within this session.
- No dividend, segment-level, or cash-flow detail beyond what's listed above was extracted; the FY2025 annual report does disclose a dividend paid during the year (SAR 49,840 thousand, per the statement of changes in equity) if that is useful for the demo app.
