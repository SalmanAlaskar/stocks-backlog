## Notes

- **Balance-sheet discrepancy:** Two sources disagree on FY2025 shareholders' equity — SAR 3,091 million (sahmcapital and premargin.com, both citing the official annual results announcement) versus SAR 3,465 million (stockanalysis.com balance sheet page). The SAR 3,091 million figure is preferred here since it traces to the official results disclosure, but this was not independently verified against the audited financial statements themselves (the Saudi Exchange / Tadawul announcement pages returned HTTP 403 and could not be fetched directly). Total assets (SAR 14,356M) and total liabilities (SAR 10,891M) for FY2025 come only from stockanalysis.com and could not be cross-checked against an official-filing source.
- **Earnings volatility / petrochemical cycle:** This company's results swing significantly with input-cost cycles. FY2024 was a net-loss year (SAR -259M), driven in part by investment losses and impairment provisions totaling SAR 345 million; FY2025 returned to profit (SAR 226M) on higher volumes (partly from the new Advanced Polyolefins Industry Company facility starting up in Q3 2025) and lower propane/propylene costs. That improvement reversed again in Q2 2026 (net loss of SAR 98M) due to a propane supply shortfall and rising feedstock costs plus new depreciation/financing charges from the same facility ramp-up — so quarter-to-quarter results have been unusually volatile across this period.
- **Quarterly EPS gap:** A standalone Q2 2026 EPS figure (as opposed to the six-month H1 2026 EPS of SAR -0.264) was not found in the sources reviewed.
- Figures are as publicly reported/estimated in company and Saudi Exchange announcements as summarized by secondary sources (Sahm Capital, Argaam-adjacent aggregators, stockanalysis.com); some Saudi Exchange source pages could not be fetched directly due to access restrictions (HTTP 403), so this summary relies on secondary reporting of those same announcements rather than the primary PDF/HTML disclosure text in all cases.
