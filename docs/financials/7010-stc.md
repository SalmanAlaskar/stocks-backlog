## Notes

- stc reports a "reported" net income figure alongside a company-described underlying/adjusted figure that excludes one-off items. For FY2025 these diverge sharply (reported net income down ~40% vs. underlying growth of +12.5%) because of a large one-time gain in FY2024 from subsidiary divestitures (TAWAL, DIC) that did not recur in FY2025. The same pattern appears in H1 2026 vs. H1 2025 (reported down ~2% vs. underlying up 6.3%), so headline year-on-year comparisons should be read carefully.
- Quarter-only Q2 2026 EPS and quarter-only Q2 2026 balance-sheet detail were not found in the sources reviewed; only cumulative six-month (H1 2026) figures were available for several line items (gross profit, operating profit, EBITDA, net profit).
- Balance-sheet figures (total assets, total liabilities) for FY2025 could not be verified consistently across sources — aggregator sites disagreed with each other and could not be cross-checked against the primary Saudi Exchange/stc filing (which required login/could not be fetched directly, HTTP 403). Total equity is reported above from a single source (Sahm Capital) and should be treated as indicative rather than fully verified.
