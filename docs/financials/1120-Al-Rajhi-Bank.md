## Notes

- As a bank, Al Rajhi Bank does not report a single top-line "revenue" figure the way a non-financial company does. Its income statement instead starts with **total operating income**, composed primarily of net financing & investment income (the Shariah-compliant equivalent of net interest income) plus fees and other income (fees, FX, and similar non-yield income). This summary reflects that distinction rather than labeling anything "revenue."
- Figures above are presented in SAR millions unless otherwise noted (thousands were not used).
- The board recommended a bonus share issuance increasing capital from SAR 40 billion to SAR 60 billion (one new share per two existing shares) around the FY2025 results announcement; this affects share count/EPS comparability going forward but the per-share figures above are as originally reported and were not retroactively adjusted for the bonus issue by the source documents reviewed.
- The 2Q 2026 earnings release notes that some prior-period balance sheet items (term financing and certain deposits) were reclassified between "Due to banks, Saudi Central Bank and other financial institutions" and "Debt securities issued and term financing" / "Customers' deposits" to reflect the Group's funding profile — so period-over-period balance sheet comparisons in the source table already reflect this reclassification.
- Quarterly figures for Q1 2026, Q3/Q4 2025, and other historical quarters were referenced in search results (e.g., 9M 2025 net profit of SAR 18,417 million) but were not independently verified against a primary source document in this research pass, so they are omitted here. Only the most recent quarter (Q2 2026) and most recent full fiscal year (FY2025) are reported in detail above, per the requested scope.
- Total liabilities and total equity were "Not found" in the initial official FY2025 press release text itself, but were confirmed via the FY2025 (4Q25) column of the official 2Q 2026 earnings release balance sheet table, which is treated as an official source.
