## Notes

- The company rebranded from "Saudi Electricity Company" (SEC) to "Saudi Energy" (SE) during this period; it remains the same legal entity and Tadawul ticker (5110). Both names refer to the same company throughout this document.
- FY2024 results included several one-off items that make year-on-year comparisons look unusually large for FY2025: a SAR 5.7 billion one-off legacy fuel/power settlement expense and a SAR 481 million one-off settlement income were both recorded in FY2024 but did not recur in FY2025. The company itself flags that, excluding these one-offs, FY2025 operating profit and net profit grew 12.4% and 7.4% respectively (rather than the 62.1% and 88.9% "as reported" figures) — this normalization should be kept in mind when using the headline growth rates.
- SEC/SE has historically carried a large, capital-intensive debt load tied to its role as the Kingdom's primary regulated transmission/distribution utility, funded via sukuk, syndicated loans, and export credit agency (ECA) financing; total debt of ~SAR 176 billion (FY2025) and continued heavy capital expenditure (FY2025 capex ~SAR 88.2 billion normalized; FY2026 guidance SAR 70–75 billion) reflect this. The company also carries a large "Mudarabah instrument" balance (~SAR 174 billion at FY2025 year-end) within total equity, which relates to a government-linked financing/equity structure specific to SEC's history and capital structure — this is unusual relative to a typical corporate balance sheet and is a reason the "EPS after deducting Mudarabah profit" (SAR 0.96 for FY2025) is the more commonly cited per-share metric rather than the pre-Mudarabah EPS (SAR 3.11).
- The most recent full quarter with a complete, directly-fetched official income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement is Q1 2026 (released May 6, 2026). The more recent H1 2026 results (published ~August 4, 2026) were only found via secondary sources (Saudi Press Agency, IndexBox, an Investing.com earnings-call transcript); the official H1/Q2 2026 earnings-release PDF on se.com.sa could not be located at a guessable URL or via search, so absolute SAR figures for several H1 2026 line items (gross profit, operating profit) and any standalone Q2-only balance sheet were not found and are marked accordingly above.
- All SAR figures above are as reported in the cited sources; some official PDF documents displayed a Saudi Riyal currency glyph that appeared as "$" when parsed as text — figures were cross-checked against multiple sources (which consistently state "SAR"/"ر.س") to confirm the currency is Saudi Riyal, not US Dollar.
