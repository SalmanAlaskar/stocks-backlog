## Notes

- **Ticker verification confirmed:** Ticker 3030 = Saudi Cement Company was independently confirmed via Argaam and TradingView company/symbol pages before any financial data was extracted. This is distinct from Saudi Ceramic Company (ticker 2040), a different, commonly confused Tadawul building-materials issuer — see `docs/financials/2040-Saudi-Ceramics.md` in this repo, which documents that same distinction from the other side.
- **Balance-sheet figures partially unverified:** Total assets (SAR 3,082 million) and total liabilities (SAR 862.28 million) for FY2025 came only from StockAnalysis.com's balance-sheet page; a second, primary-source confirmation (e.g., the audited annual report PDF) was not retrieved in this session. Internal consistency check passes (liabilities + equity ≈ total assets: 862.28 + 2,219.9 ≈ 3,082.2), and total equity is independently corroborated by the company's own IR press release, so these figures are likely accurate but are flagged as lower-confidence than the income-statement figures, which are corroborated across the official company announcement, Argaam, and StockAnalysis.com.
- **Profit decline driver (FY2025):** The company attributed the FY2025 net income decline primarily to lower average selling prices (despite higher sales volumes), decreased other income, a lower profit share from an associate, and higher zakat expense, per its official annual-results announcement.
- **Q4 2025 note:** Standalone Q4 2025 net profit was reported at SAR 106.5 million (down 11.7% YoY from SAR 120.6 million in Q4 2024), a sequential improvement from Q3 2025's SAR 53.23 million, per Argaam's Q1 2026 report commentary (used only as supporting context, not reported as a headline figure above since it fell outside the "most recent quarter/year" scope requested).
- **No dividend, segment, or cash-flow detail** was extracted for this summary; only income-statement and partial balance-sheet data were in scope.
- **Currency:** All figures are in Saudi Riyals (SAR), as reported by the cited sources; figures are stated in millions unless noted otherwise.
